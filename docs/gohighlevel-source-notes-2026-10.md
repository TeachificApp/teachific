# GoHighLevel source notes — October 2026

This note preserves external research used for the Course360 high-level feature-gap audit.

## Official sources and verified capability claims

1. **GoHighLevel platform overview** — https://www.gohighlevel.com/
   - Positions HighLevel as an AI-powered business operating system for capturing, nurturing, closing, retaining, and reactivating customers.
   - Lists CRM, forms/surveys/quizzes, websites/funnels/landing pages, webinar funnels, chat/conversation AI, call tracking, inbound SMS/social DMs, social planner, sales pipelines, workflows, calendars, appointment reminders, payments, invoice/order forms, memberships/courses, upsells, loyalty, review requests, affiliate management, communities, and multi-channel broadcast campaigns.

2. **GoHighLevel CRM overview** — https://www.gohighlevel.com/crm
   - Describes visual sales pipelines, AI-powered conversations, workflow automation, a unified inbox across SMS, email, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, and live chat, website/funnel builders, reputation management, and agency client sub-accounts.
   - States Agency Pro provides SaaS mode, automated sub-account creation, reseller-style rebilling, user/agent reporting, and advanced API access.

3. **GoHighLevel white-label CRM overview** — https://www.gohighlevel.com/white-label-crm
   - Describes custom logos, colors, fonts, menu items, custom domains, branded desktop/mobile experience, platform branding, and rollout to multiple client sub-accounts.
   - Also describes bundled client-facing communication, scheduling, social planning, reputation management, document handling, invoicing, and point-of-sale capabilities.

4. **Workflow introduction** — https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000002445-introduction-to-workflows-and-automations
   - Defines an automation model built from event triggers and subsequent actions.
   - Gives booking, form submission, and contact reply as trigger examples; email sending, contact updates, and lead assignment as action examples.
   - Documents starting from recipes/templates or from scratch, and testing a workflow before publishing.

5. **Conversation AI appointment booking** — https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000000210-appointment-booking-in-conversation-ai
   - Documents calendar-based AI booking, contact capture, availability selection, single- or multi-calendar routing, cancellations/rescheduling, service calendars, and post-booking workflow triggers.

6. **Online reputation management overview** — https://www.gohighlevel.com/online-reputation-management-software
   - Describes automated email/SMS review requests after appointments or sales, a central review dashboard, AI-assisted review replies, sentiment/reputation analytics, review disputes, and unified inbox treatment for review interactions.

7. **Webhook integration guide** — https://marketplace.gohighlevel.com/docs/webhook/WebhookIntegrationGuide/
   - Documents signed, event-based delivery for contact, opportunity, task, appointment, invoice, product, association, location, and user events.
   - Recommends signature verification, idempotency keyed by webhook ID, rapid acknowledgment, and asynchronous processing; describes OAuth app scopes and event subscriptions.
